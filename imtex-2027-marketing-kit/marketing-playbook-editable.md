A practical campaign for Crystal magnetic and non-magnetic products: help buyers identify the relevant product family, share their application and arrange a useful sales conversation. Use the enclosed visuals for awareness and the enquiry cards to capture requirements.

# Lead capture and follow-up process

## Minimum enquiry record
Date | Contact name | Company | Email or phone | Contact permission/preferred channel | Source | Product family | Application | Material | Dimensions or load details | Quantity | Timing | Missing information | Owner | Next action | Due date | Status | Quote reference | Outcome.

## Qualify without pressure
Ask: What are you trying to hold, lift or inspect? What is the component? Which operation matters? What problem are you solving? When do you need a solution? Who should join the technical discussion?

## Priority definitions
Priority A: clear requirement, named contact, timeframe and agreed next step. Priority B: relevant application but missing technical or timing details. Priority C: general information request without a current application. These are internal triage categories, not claims about buying probability.

## Follow-up rules
A: contact by the agreed date; if none was agreed, aim for the next business day. B: send the missing-information request and agree a follow-up date. C: send relevant information once and ask whether they want future updates. Do not promise a quotation date before technical review.

## Track weekly
Enquiries by source; qualified enquiries; meetings held; quotations sent; orders received; open actions past due. Qualification rate = qualified enquiries / total enquiries. Quote-to-order rate = orders / quotations, using a consistent cohort and review period. Record actual spend before calculating cost per qualified enquiry. No campaign targets or conversion claims are assumed in this kit.

## Attribution
Use source labels such as IMTEX stall, dealer name, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and company website. Ask how the buyer found the company. If public web analytics is added later, define tracking and data handling before launch.
