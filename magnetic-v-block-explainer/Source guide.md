## Suggested 40-second edit

1. **0:00–0:04** — Open on the switch turning or the shaft coming free. Voiceover idea — “How do you hold a round steel shaft with one turn of a switch?”
2. **0:04–0:14** — Run the animation. Give each action a beat so the viewer sees the shaft seat, hold, and release.
3. **0:14–0:23** — Use the labeled diagram to show the two faces of the V and where the switch sits.
4. **0:23–0:34** — Cut to grinding, drilling, and inspection B-roll as you name the applications. Use the F-IMG photo when describing general V-block fixturing.
5. **0:34–0:40** — Close on a real bench demonstration if you have a block. Film one continuous shot of the same steel shaft being seated, held, and lifted free.
